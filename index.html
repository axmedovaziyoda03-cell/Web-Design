How development through Alcaine works

#1Assemble the right team

We handle all aspects of hiring and choosing the right team that you deem best for skill, expertise, and needs.

#2Tech architecture

We design formation plans that structure the code architecture, allowing your team to move fast and stay maintainable.

#3Code reviews

Code reviews before merges ensure the team is delivering robust, clean, high-quality code with a consistent standard.

#4Sprint planning

Sprint planning is a collaborative planning effort where the team commits to what can be delivered in the sprint.

#5Standups & weekly demo

Standups, weekly demos, and weekly reviews keep everyone in sync on the status and what can be shared.

#6Iterative delivery

We divide the implementation process into small increments so that structural changes rather than a huge codebase.
